## Purpose

This Stage 1 archive timestamps the study design before formal study outcomes
are generated or inspected. It freezes the computational core and the planned
human-validation modules while preserving explicit execution gates for evidence
that cannot truthfully be supplied before personnel, API, ethics, and software
checks occur.
