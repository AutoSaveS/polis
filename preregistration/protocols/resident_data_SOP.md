# Resident participant-data SOP

Status: proposed for ethics review; final procedures must match the approval.

1. Collect only the minimum approved fields. Disable unnecessary IP, GPS,
   device fingerprinting, advertising cookies, and analytics where the platform
   permits. Never request an exact address, full postcode/ZIP code, or precise
   home/work coordinates.
2. Store contact details, consent evidence, withdrawal requests, and the
   participant-code linkage separately from responses. Restrict linkage access
   to the named PI and student researcher unless the final approval states a
   narrower or different access list.
3. Export data promptly to the institutionally approved storage location and
   delete platform copies according to the approved schedule. Record export and
   deletion events in an audit log.
4. Before any cross-border transfer, remove direct identifiers, minimise quasi-
   identifiers, encrypt in transit and at rest, and use only the route named in
   the final approval and data-transfer assessment.
5. Do not upload raw, pseudonymised, location-linked, or free-text resident data
   to a generative-AI or other unapproved external service. Statistical and
   qualitative processing occurs in the approved controlled environment.
6. Use the linkage file to action an eligible withdrawal within the approved
   withdrawal window. Do not retain the reason unless the participant freely
   supplies it and the approval permits retention.
7. Keep raw data immutable. Cleaning creates a derived dataset with scripted
   transformations, exclusion flags, codebook version, and an audit trail. No
   missing primary fidelity value is imputed.

No missing primary fidelity value is imputed.
8. Share only fully anonymised, disclosure-reviewed fields where specific
   consent and all applicable approvals permit it. Never publish direct
   identifiers, linkage files, verbatim text that identifies a person, or
   precise residential location.
9. The retention period and deletion date are inserted only from the approved
   resident application; they are not inferred from the expert approval.
