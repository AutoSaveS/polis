# Expert participant-data SOP

This SOP applies only to the approved independent expert-review component.

1. Collect ratings online only after active consent. Collect no sensitive or
   special-category data and disable unnecessary IP, location, device, and
   tracking fields where the selected platform permits.
2. Store names, email addresses, consent records, and reviewer-code linkage in
   a restricted XJTLU BOX location accessible only to John Moraros and Jiawei
   Tong. Store rating data separately under reviewer codes.
3. Export responses from the collection platform to XJTLU BOX and delete the
   platform copy after the collection phase, as stated in the approved form.
4. Before any cross-border transfer, remove direct identifiers, minimise
   fields, encrypt transfer and storage, and restrict access to named research
   team members. Use only approved secure channels such as XJTLU BOX or SFTP.
5. Do not upload raw, pseudonymised, or free-text participant data to
   generative-AI systems. Run the frozen statistical script in the approved
   controlled environment.
6. Process a withdrawal received before anonymisation and aggregation by using
   the linkage file to remove the participant's identifiers and linked ratings.
   Record the withdrawal without retaining its reason.
7. Securely delete identifiable data three years after study completion.
   Deposit fully anonymised data in OSF or Zenodo only when specific repository
   consent is present and disclosure review confirms that professional
   background combinations cannot identify a reviewer.
