## Finding

Status: `NOT_CONFIRMED_BY_APPROVAL_RECORD`.

The three local PDFs must remain in `ethics/draft/` and must not be described as
approved attachments. Before expert recruitment, the PI must obtain and archive
one of the following:

1. the exact files downloaded from the ethics system together with hashes and
   approval-record evidence; or
2. written confirmation from XJTLU that the intended files and wording are
   covered by the approval.

The confirmation must also resolve the institutional complaints contact, exact
bilingual wording and scale anchors, approved collection platform, and whether
the 8 July uploads were incorporated into or accepted under the 6 July approval.
Any substantive mismatch requires ethics guidance or an amendment.
